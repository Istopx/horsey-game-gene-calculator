Tips for best results
• Fence or group all helices so they're spread out and not heavily overlapping
• The helix number labels (0–19) must be visible — the AI uses these to identify each helix
• Fullscreen screenshot = more pixels = better accuracy
• If helices overlap a lot, separate them into 2 screenshots and scan twice
• Any bases the AI is unsure about will be flagged — you can fix them manually in the Analyzer tab
